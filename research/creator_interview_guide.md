# Creator interview guide

Goal: talk to 2–3 people who have published (or tried to publish) a Fortnite island, so the PRD
can rest on firsthand input, not only forum posts. Even one conversation helps.

## Is it worth it?

Yes. The posting asks the intern to "talk to operators and partners to turn workflow pain points
into actionable requirements." Saying "I talked to three creators and one of them changed my
priorities" is a stronger interview answer than anything in the research file.

If nobody replies, that's fine. The PRD will say it rests on secondary research only, and
"what I'd do with real creator access" becomes an interview answer.

## Where to find creators

- Friends who build Zone Wars or other maps (ask your squad who they know)
- r/FortniteCreative and r/UEFN (read each subreddit's rules on surveys/requests first)
- The Epic Developer Community forums
- Public creator Discord servers (only post in channels meant for questions or feedback)

## Message to send (edit it into your own words)

> Hi! I'm an MIS student at San Jose State building an independent portfolio project about the
> island publishing process (not affiliated with Epic). Have you published an island? I'd love
> 10–15 minutes, or written answers to 5 quick questions, about what was hard. No names will
> appear in the project unless you want them to.

## Questions (pick 5 if it's a written reply)

1. Walk me through the last time you published or updated an island. Where did it slow you down?
2. Have you ever been rejected? What was the reason, and how long did it take you to figure out the fix?
3. Before you submit, how do you check your listing (title, description, thumbnail, rating)? A checklist? Memory? Nothing?
4. How did you answer the age-rating questionnaire? Was anything unclear?
5. How long does review usually take for you, and how do you know where it stands?
6. If you could change one thing about publishing, what would it be?
7. (Show the prototype later, if they're up for it) Would this check have caught your last rejection?

## What to write down

For each person: date, how many islands they've published, and their answers in their own
words. Note **surprises**, the things that contradict `pain_points.md`. Those are the most valuable.

Keep notes in `research/creator_interviews.md` (initials only, no names or handles unless they agree).
